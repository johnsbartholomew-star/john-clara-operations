# New Store Opening Playbook — People Readiness Sample

Prepared 2026-09-13. **Illustrative portfolio sample only.** This is not client work, does not reproduce an employer's confidential process, and requires adaptation to the client's organization, labor rules, systems, and approvals.

## Purpose and outcome

Give a Store Leader one operating view of recruiting, onboarding, training, scheduling, and opening-week readiness. By opening day, critical roles are filled or covered, required onboarding and training are complete, access and equipment are ready, and every gap has an owner and decision path.

## Illustrative timeline

| Timing | Outcome | Store Leader action | Owner | Evidence | Escalation trigger |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| T-12 to T-10 | Staffing plan approved | Validate roles, headcount, hours, sequence, and constraints | Retail Operations | Approved plan | Headcount or payroll unresolved by T-10 |
| T-10 to T-6 | Hiring pipeline supports dates | Track interviews, offers, acceptance risk, and backup candidates | HR / Recruiting | Weekly tracker | Critical role lacks a viable finalist |
| T-8 to T-4 | Training plan confirmed | Map role learning, trainer capacity, practice, and sign-off | Learning / Operations | Training calendar | Training cannot finish before opening |
| T-6 to T-2 | Pre-start actions completed | Monitor status in approved HR systems; keep personal data out of this tracker | HR | Completion status | Hire may miss the permitted start date |
| T-4 to T-1 | Team scheduled and equipped | Confirm coverage, access, equipment, attendance, and contingencies | Store Leader | Readiness tracker | Coverage falls below approved need |
| T-1 to T0 | Opening roster executable | Review readiness, assign roles, and close or escalate gaps | Store Leader | Signed review | Gap threatens safe or customer-ready opening |
| T0 to T+2 weeks | Team stabilizes | Run daily reviews, coach, and assign unresolved issues | Store Leader | Issue log | Repeated failure materially affects operations |

## Readiness checks

### Staffing

- Required roles and headcount are understood.
- Critical roles are filled or have approved contingency coverage.
- Opening-week schedule covers operations, training, setup, and recovery.
- Overtime, travel, borrowed labor, or temporary coverage has approval.

### Onboarding and access

- Required steps are complete in the approved HR system.
- POS, scheduling, and communications access have owners and dates.
- Uniforms, keys, devices, or authorized equipment are ready or tracked.
- Passwords never appear in the readiness tracker.

### Training

- Each role has required learning and a completion method.
- Trainers, materials, systems, locations, and practice time are confirmed.
- Missed learning has a catch-up owner and date.

## Exception logic

**Critical-role gap:** show the operational impact, actions already taken, and dated coverage options; escalate for an authorized staffing or opening-plan decision.

**Training not complete:** separate mandatory readiness from post-opening learning; add training capacity, change assignments, or delay independent task ownership through the approved decision maker.

**System access failure:** verify the request and owner, never share credentials, temporarily reassign the task, and escalate if opening readiness is affected.

## Completion standard

Client approval is required for the real timeline, ownership, HR/compliance steps, evidence systems, and escalation authorities. A future Store Leader should be able to identify the next action and owner for every unresolved gap.
