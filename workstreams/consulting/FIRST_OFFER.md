# First consulting offer — review draft

Not published. Pricing, demand, and delivery capacity remain unvalidated.

## Offer

Turn one recurring retail task into a clear SOP and usable manager checklist.

## Deliverables

- Review one existing workflow and identify gaps.
- Create one customized SOP of up to three pages.
- Create one one-page completion checklist.
- Incorporate one consolidated feedback round.

## Boundaries

This is one workflow, not a business overhaul, software implementation, ongoing management service, or legal/HR compliance engagement. No revenue or savings guarantees.
